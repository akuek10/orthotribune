Shoulder Suture Anchor Economics & Value Frontier

Evaluating the relationship between construct market selling prices ($ USD), secondary failure/revision rates (%), and clinical improvement on the ASES score (represented by bubble diameter).

Bankart Instability: Anchor Density vs. Material Equivalence

Transitioning to 3–4 knotless/all-suture anchors ($2,450 USD) drives recurrence down to 7.8%. Clinical meta-analyses confirm all-suture is non-inferior to solid PEEK; the lower failure is primarily driven by standardized ≥3 fixation points and preserving glenoid rim bone stock.

Rotator Cuff Repairs: Mechanical Compression vs. ROHI Ceilings

Transosseous-equivalent Knotless SutureBridge ($3,250 USD) drops structural failure to 9.5% in general/medium tears. However, in high-ROHI patients (age >65, tear >3 cm, Goutallier ≥2), mechanical footprint compression alone reaches a biological limit, with re-tears climbing to 25–40% without augmentation.
