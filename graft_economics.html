Rotator Cuff Augmentation: Health Economics & Clinical PROM Value

An evidence-based comparison of hospital graft acquisition costs (USD), downstream revision cost avoidance, and statistically validated Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (ASES score gain).

PROM Clinical Impact (The MCID Benchmark):
Bubble size corresponds to magnitude of structural re-tear reduction. Human Acellular Dermal Matrix (hADM) is the only graft class that consistently exceeds the Minimal Clinically Important Difference (MCID of +6.4 points) on the ASES functional scale in Level I/II randomized cohorts (+10.5 points, p < 0.001). First-generation porcine xenografts produced negative net PROM scores due to persistent inflammatory synovitis.
Episode-of-Care Economic Offsets:
Because a revision rotator cuff repair or secondary Reverse Shoulder Arthroplasty (RSA) costs between $22,000 and $35,000 USD per episode, deploying a $4,500 hADM allograft in high-risk patients yields an expected revision savings of ~$6,360 USD per treated patient (reducing structural failure from 38% down to 11.5%). This produces a net positive economic return of +$1,860 USD per index case for the healthcare payer.
