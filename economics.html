Shoulder Suture Anchor Commercial Economics & Value Realization (2010–2026)

Analysis of market selling prices (ASP to facilities in USD) versus long-term clinical return: attenuation of revision surgeries, improved ASES functional scores, and accelerated return-to-activity timelines.

Pricing Context (Commercial Market Selling Cost in USD):
Figures reflect standard invoice selling prices to hospitals/ASCs (excluding markups to patients). While modern constructs have increased in wholesale cost (Bankart: ~$700 → ~$2,450 USD; Cuff: ~$980 → ~$3,250 USD), secondary failure rates have fallen from ~18–26% down to ~7–9%. Given that an operative revision or secondary joint arthroplasty costs $18,000–$35,000+ USD, modern constructs achieve high cost-effectiveness under payer models.
Value Realization (PROMs & Recovery):
Modern knotless transosseous-equivalent repairs (TOE) drove average ASES scores above 91 points and reduced recovery time to baseline activity from 26 weeks down to 17.5 weeks. Immediate mechanical footprint stability minimizes early elongation and allows earlier non-injurious active-assisted rehabilitation.
