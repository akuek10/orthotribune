Shoulder Suture Anchor Dynamics & Adoption (2010–2026)

Longitudinal clinical preference trends across Bankart labral repairs and Rotator Cuff footprint fixation, benchmarked against anchor diameter sizing, ultimate pullout load-to-failure (N), and native physiological tissue thresholds.

Biomechanical Takeaway: Circle size corresponds to clinical market share. Even small 1.4–1.8 mm all-suture anchors (240–310 N) comfortably exceed the native inferior glenohumeral labral complex failure threshold (~180 N), confirming that bone conservation on the glenoid rim can be achieved without sacrificing fixation load capacity.
15-Year Longitudinal Shift: Highlights the near-total abandonment of 1st-generation pure PLLA implants following clinical evidence of osteolytic cyst formation, alongside the concurrent shift toward osteoconductive biocomposites and all-suture constructs.
