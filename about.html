About the Project

Where Orthopaedic Evidence Meets Spreadsheet Reality

Orthopaedic literature is rich in biomechanical pullout curves, cadaveric test rigs, and Level I p-values - yet remarkably quiet when someone in a hospital Value Analysis Committee asks what a marginal healing gain actually costs per avoided revision. OrthoTribune is a personal sandbox for translating dense clinical literature, implant microarchitecture, and regional market dynamics into interactive tools you can stress-test without a brochure in sight.

1. Unvarnished Literature

Separating established Level-I consensus from cadaveric nuance - explicitly flagging where implant hierarchies remain unproven and where biomechanical differences stay on the bench.

2. Episode & VAC Economics

Moving past unit ASP sticker shock to model total episode cost, selective risk stratification, and break-even thresholds calibrated to real-world reoperation multipliers.

3. SEA & Greater China Lens

Mapping procedural delivery, public-private splits, and reimbursement pathways across regions.
