Shoulder Suture Anchor Economics: Commercial Cost vs. Structural Integrity

A discrete comparative evaluation of facility acquisition prices (commercial wholesale ASP in USD) against published structural failure rates across randomized trials and multi-center cohorts.

Bankart Fixation: Anchor Number vs. Material

Recurrence dropped from ~18.5% to ≤10% when clinical practice shifted from under-dosing (<3 anchors) to placing ≥3 points of fixation. In comparative trials (Barber, Willemot), all-suture anchors show clinical equivalency / non-inferiority to solid PEEK; their adoption is driven by glenoid rim bone stock conservation rather than superior fixation strength.

Rotator Cuff: Structural Healing vs. PROMs

Transosseous-equivalent double-row constructs significantly improve structural tendon integrity (MRI healing) in large tears (>3 cm) over single-row repairs. However, meta-analyses (Duquin, Hein) show that when tendons heal, patient-reported outcome scores (ASES, Constant) are often similar. Furthermore, mechanical constructs alone cannot prevent re-tears in patients with advanced fatty degeneration (ROHI high-risk).
