Commercial Positioning Across SEA & Greater China Archetypes: Mature hubs (Singapore, Taiwan, Hong Kong) evaluate new sports technologies through total episode economics—balancing device acquisition cost against OR efficiency, day-surgery readiness, and revision avoidance. High-growth Southeast Asian markets (Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia) reward tiered product portfolios (pairing flagship implants for insured/self-pay tiers with cost-effective, reusable-instrument platforms for public/bundled tiers) backed by hands-on arthroscopy training.
